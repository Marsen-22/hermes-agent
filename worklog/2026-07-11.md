### Agentic Benchmark — All 18 Models

Ran a unified agentic benchmark across the full model fleet via LiteLLM proxy → LM Studio. 5 categories × 2 prompts per model, weighted scoring (Tool 0.25, Plan 0.25, Struct 0.25, Instr 0.15, Reason 0.10).
